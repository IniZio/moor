The portal is installed as {{ include "portal.fullname" . }}.

Reach it from your machine, with the header the sign-in proxy would set:
  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} port-forward svc/{{ include "portal.fullname" . }} 8080:{{ .Values.service.port }}
  curl -H '{{ .Values.portal.userHeader }}: you@example.org' http://127.0.0.1:8080/tasks

The portal trusts {{ .Values.portal.userHeader }} without checking it. Only the sign-in proxy may reach this Service.
